# Demo credits

`synesthesia-voice.wav` is 18 seconds of John F. Kennedy's inaugural address, 20 January 1961: "ask not what your country can do for you". It is a US government work. The John F. Kennedy Presidential Library lists its recording, [JFKWHA-001](https://www.jfklibrary.org/asset-viewer/archives/jfkwha-001), as public domain.

The audio comes from the library-credited copy on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:JFK_inaugural_address.ogg). It was cut from 12:36 to 12:54, faded out, normalised and converted to mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit. Its use here implies no endorsement.
